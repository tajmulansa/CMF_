# Observed in our own Vox clips (O = measured/observed by us)

Sample: 2 clips, 5.4 s total, one episode (archival black-and-white montage). Treat as a first data point, not a style law. Details: `references/vox/vintage-transaction-1/` and `-2/` (notes.md + analysis.json). Frames: `references/frames/<slug>/`, sheets: `references/sheets/<slug>/`.

## What the clips confirm, change or leave open
| Earlier rule | Result | Status |
|---|---|---|
| T4 compose at 24 fps | Both clips are 24 fps content (2-3 pulldown inside a 60 fps file, measured 23.7-23.9). | Supported |
| T3 graphics step at 12 fps | Archival footage moves at full 24 fps. The watermark and captions are static, so nothing here tests stepped graphics. | NOT observed (still tutorial-only) |
| P6 hard cuts by default | All 4 picture changes in these clips use a dark transition: a 0.167 s (4 frames at 24 fps) dip to dark grey, or a short slide with a dark gutter. No plain hard cut. | Contradicted for this montage |
| P3 shot length (median about 3.6 s) | Measured shots: 2.00 s and 1.58 s (complete), plus 0.42 s and 0.33 s partials. Faster than the vendor median. Four shots only. | Weak evidence of faster pacing |
| T9/T10 paper and grain | Title card photo washed to cream/paper tone; dark dips carry faint dust specks and a faint grid (O, unconfirmed). Archival footage has its own film grain. | Weakly supported |
| S3 silence before a reveal | Audio is digital silence for the first 0.5 s (under the title card and black), then continues through the dips. | Partly supported |

## New observations to add to the skill
- O1. Shot change = dip through flat dark grey (about RGB 29, 29, 29 = #1d1d1d, not pure black), 4 frames at 24 fps, with the corner watermark staying on screen. Pure black appears only after the title card (about 0.17 s).
- O2. Corner watermark: small white serif "Vox" top right, constant across shots and dips; fades in out of the black.
- O3. Subtitle style: white, bold-ish sans, two lines, centred, in the lower third, subtle shadow. The text change lags the picture cut by about 0.25 s.
- O4. Archival shots drift slowly (a few percent scale over 1.5-2 s) instead of sitting still. Magnitudes are approximate (phase-correlation/ECC estimates on crowd texture).
- O5. Title card: series logo and name in serif caps over a heavily washed-out photo (cream/off-white overlay).
- O6. Audio does not drop out at the dips in this sample.

## Open questions this sample cannot answer
Colour/graphic scenes, maps, charts, annotations (highlighter, underline), lower thirds, music vs voice levels, and how often each transition type is used over a full video. Next clips should cover those.
